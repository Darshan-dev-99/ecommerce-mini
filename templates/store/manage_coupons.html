How coupons apply

Coupons are validated server-side at cart apply and again inside the checkout transaction, so a race between two tabs can never double-discount an order. The discount is applied before GST is computed, and delivery charges are calculated on the post-discount subtotal.

Retiring a coupon

Deleting a coupon that was already used by past orders does not delete history — it is deactivated instead and stays visible on those invoices.
